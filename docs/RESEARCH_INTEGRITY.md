# Research Integrity Rules

- Do not report fabricated experimental results.
- Distinguish a hypothesis from a verified result.
- Distinguish claimed scope from verified scope.
- Do not call "no observed advantage" a proof of security.
- Do not say an algorithm is broken or secure merely because the application contains a record.
- Mark AI-generated claim content with `Origin=AI` or `HYBRID`.
- AI output alone cannot promote assurance from L0 to L1/L2/L3.
- Keep binary evidence outside Google Sheets.
- Treat Google Sheets as a persistence layer, not as an immutable forensic ledger.
- Use synthetic/controlled data during development.
